ACME Corp – Travel & Expense Policy (excerpt)

1. Booking
   Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance through the corporate
   travel portal. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours.

2. Accommodation
   Nightly cap: 220 USD in tier-1 cities, 150 USD elsewhere. Receipts are
   mandatory for every claim above 25 USD.

3. Meals
   Per-diem is 65 USD. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

4. Ground transport
   Ride-hailing and public transport are reimbursable. Car rental requires
   line-manager approval before the trip.

5. Submission
   Expenses must be filed within 30 days of the trip ending. Late claims need
   finance director sign-off.
